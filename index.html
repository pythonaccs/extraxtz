A Reminder for You

You are not too far gone. You are not forgotten. God still restores, still forgives, still strengthens, and still leads His people.

“Draw nigh to God, and he will draw nigh to you.”

James 4:8

Biblical Topics

Philippians 4:6-7

“Be careful for nothing; but in every thing by prayer and supplication... let your requests be made known unto God.”

Bring your worry to God. His peace can guard your heart and mind.

John 14:27

“Peace I leave with you, my peace I give unto you...”

The peace Jesus gives is deeper than the peace the world offers.

2 Corinthians 12:9

“My grace is sufficient for thee: for my strength is made perfect in weakness.”

God’s strength shows up even when you feel like you have none left.

Philippians 4:13

“I can do all things through Christ which strengtheneth me.”

Your strength does not have to come from yourself. Christ strengthens you.

Isaiah 41:10

“Fear thou not; for I am with thee...”

God’s presence is greater than fear.

2 Timothy 1:7

“For God hath not given us the spirit of fear; but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind.”

Fear is not your identity. God gives power, love, and a sound mind.

Philippians 4:8

“Whatsoever things are true, honest, just, pure, lovely… think on these things.”

Focus your mind on godly things instead of fear and negativity.

Romans 8:6

“To be spiritually minded is life and peace.”

A mind centered on God produces peace.

Ephesians 4:26-27

“Be ye angry, and sin not… Neither give place to the devil.”

Uncontrolled anger can become spiritually dangerous.

James 1:20

“The wrath of man worketh not the righteousness of God.”

Human rage does not produce God's righteousness.

Proverbs 16:18

“Pride goeth before destruction, and an haughty spirit before a fall.”

Pride blinds people and leads them away from humility.

James 4:6

“God resisteth the proud, but giveth grace unto the humble.”

God gives grace to the humble.

2 Corinthians 10:5

“Casting down imaginations… bringing every thought into obedience to Christ.”

The battlefield often begins in the mind.

Ephesians 6:11

“Put on the whole armour of God…”

God gives believers spiritual armor.

Hebrews 11:1

“Faith is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen.”

Faith trusts God even when you cannot fully see ahead.

2 Corinthians 5:7

“For we walk by faith, not by sight.”

Christians live by trust in God.
